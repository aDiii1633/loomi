What Loomi is

Every app you share is public. Loomi is just for two.

Not a social feed, not a group chat. Loomi is a couple's own small world — one thread, one calendar, one vault, one streak, built around exactly two people.

Privacy & security

Private by design. Not by promise.

Your relationship is yours. Here's exactly what that means today.

Secure sign-in

Accounts are authenticated through Clerk, a dedicated identity provider — Loomi never stores your password.

Couple-level isolation

Database rules enforce that only the two people in a couple can ever read or write that couple's data.

PIN + biometric vault

The Vault is locked behind a PIN and, where supported, your device's fingerprint or face unlock.

Location is opt-in

Location is off by default. Turning it on — and off — is always your choice, in the app, in one tap.

Being precise matters to us: Loomi does not currently use end-to-end encryption or a zero-knowledge architecture. What's described above is what's actually implemented today — we'll update this page as that changes.
